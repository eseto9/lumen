LUMEN

Your light is your life · 40 rounds until dawn

How to Play

  • You are a firefly in an endless night. Your light is your health, your vision and your ammo — and it slowly fades.
  • Hold FLARE to charge, release to burst. Bigger bursts reach further but burn more light. Destroyed shadows drop golden sparks — collect them to refuel.
  • Multi-kills multiply: the 2nd shadow in one burst scores ×2, the 3rd ×3… Let them gather — if you dare.
  • Shadows hunt the brightest light. Shining dim keeps you hidden, and kills made under 25% light score ×2 (Last Light).
  • Don't camp. Linger in one area and the Gloom gathers: shadows rise from the ground around you and your light drains faster. Sparks appear away from you — roam to refuel.
  • 40 rounds. Each round is a wave — clear every shadow to finish it. Between rounds, choose 1 of 3 boons (move + flare, keys 1–3, or click/tap). Boons stack all run and come in four rarities: Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary relics.
  • Synergies: some boons pair up — own both and a bonus unlocks (e.g. Wide Bloom + Quick Wick = Wildfire, Chain Spark + Starfire = Storm Front). Cards that would complete a pair glow gold with ⚡.
  • Bosses every 5th round: Umbra, The Maw, The Weaver, Eclipse, Hydra… and The Long Night at round 40. Each boss drops a relic — a legendary boon, offered right away and unlocked forever for future runs.
  • Co-op has its own boons: Soul Link, Hearth, Guardian, Twin Flare, Shared Glow, Beacon. Fallen friends rekindle between rounds. In versus, kills give XP and level-ups drop crystals to fly into.
  • Powerups glow through the dark: Prism, Ward, Overcharge, Moonbeam, Honeylight, Swarm. Grab them before they fade (or before your rival does).
  • Shadows bite hard and grow tougher every round. New kinds join as you go — swarmlings in packs, spitters that fire bolts (your bursts destroy bolts). Survive round 40 to see the dawn.
  • Co-op: stay close to share warmth (slower fade). Flare at the same time with overlapping bursts to fire a Resonance beam between you. Fly to a faded friend to rekindle them.
  • Versus: two minutes, most points wins. Your bursts steal light from rivals and knock them away; your kills drop sparks only you can grab for a moment.

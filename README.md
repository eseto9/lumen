# LUMEN

**Play it here: https://eseto9.github.io/lumen/**

A firefly survival roguelike that runs in your browser — one HTML file, no installs.

Your light is your health, your vision and your ammo. Hold to charge a burst of light, release to wipe out the shadows closing in — but every burst costs light, and the shadows hunt whoever shines brightest.

## Features
- **40-round roguelike** — waves grow every round, new shadow types join as you go, and a boss guards every 5th round, ending with *The Long Night*.
- **Draft a boon after every round** — Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary boons that stack all run, plus **synergies** that unlock when you own the right pair.
- **Relics** — each boss drops a legendary relic that stays unlocked for future runs.
- **Co-op & versus** — up to 4 players on one device (keyboards, gamepads, touch), bots to fill empty seats, and 2-player online play over peer-to-peer WebRTC (no server — swap two copy/paste codes).
- Procedural lighting, particles and generative music — everything is drawn and composed in code.

## Controls
| | |
|---|---|
| Move | WASD / Arrows · left stick · drag (touch) · mouse |
| Flare | hold Space / Enter · Ⓐ / RT · hold right side (touch) · left click |
| Pick a boon | hover/move + flare · keys 1–3 · click / tap |
| Pause / Mute | Esc or P · M |

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in any modern browser. For online play between two tabs or devices, serve the folder (e.g. `python -m http.server`) and use **Online → Host / Join**.
